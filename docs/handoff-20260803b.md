## What was wrong, and what it actually was

The previous handoff proposed that artwork was bound to old channel numbers,
with a specific test. **The test was not needed: the hypothesis was wrong.**
Two independent faults were present at once, which is why single-cause
theories kept half-explaining the symptom.

**1. NextPVR prefers `.jpg` over `.png`.** 129 channels had both a provider
`.jpg` placeholder and a curated `.png`. NextPVR served the placeholder in all
69 cases where they differed, and Jellyfin faithfully cached it. Confirmed
empirically 69/69 and in the decompiled `NShared.dll` — `StreamIcon` tests
`File.Exists(<name>.jpg)` first and branches straight to serving it.

**2. A stale Jellyfin image cache** on 36 channels. This is what produced the
visible "channel 300 is showing LAFF TV's logo" symptom.

The donor/receiver pattern that looked like number-keying was a coincidence of
which channels had been touched in which order.

### Storage — the orphan leak, and what settles where

Live TV stores no stream data; CT 112's recordings directory is empty. What
fills the disk is guide artwork, and it was **leaking**: every guide refresh
recreates programme items under fresh GUIDs and abandons their metadata
directories. CT 112 went 7 GB -> 34 GB across four refreshes in one day.

Pruned 2026-08-03: 16,473 orphaned directories on CT 112 (9.8 GB) and 24,068
on production (6.6 GB). `jellyfin-metadata-prune.timer` now runs weekly,
Sundays 06:30. The pruner refuses to act if it cannot establish what is
referenced — an empty referenced set would mean "delete everything".

Settled composition, CT 112 (single 59 GB root):

| | Size | Grows with |
|---|---|---|
| Jellyfin guide artwork | 9.0 GB | programmes — ~367 KB each x 24,531 |
| NextPVR show artwork | 2.7 GB | distinct show *names* — bounded, not per-refresh |
| Channel logos | 24 MB | the thing that took two days, and it is a rounding error |
| DBs, logs, backups | ~1 GB | |

Production is not tight: `/srv/media-core` is a separate **984 GB volume with
796 GB free**; its 32 GB root sits at a third. The 96 GB of metadata there is
the VOD library, not Live TV (Live TV is 3.9 GB).
**These are correct as they are.** Counting them as defects overstates the
problem tenfold.
